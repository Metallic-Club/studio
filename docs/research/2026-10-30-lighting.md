# Metallic lighting: research, tiers, creator API and presets (issue #37)

Status: planned. Research and prototypes only; nothing here is implemented in `metallic_render`.

Goal from the issue: lighting that beats Roblox Future and heads toward Unreal, at 60 fps on an RTX 3060 Ti
(1080p High) and 30 fps on Intel Iris Xe (Low). Companion docs: render styles
(`docs/research/2026-10-30-rendering-styles-vfx-animation.md`, "render-styles" below) and performance
(`docs/research/2026-10-30-performance-and-scale.md`). Their tiers, budgets and M1 hooks stand; this doc adds the
lighting detail and changes one call (the Realistic tonemapper, section 3.10).

Every claim carries a mark. **Sourced** means a page I fetched (listed in References). **Judgment** means my
reasoning or a number I chose. Choices I made for the owner say "assistant's call".

Artifacts in this PR:

- `docs/research/lighting-shaders/*.wgsl`: 12 WGSL prototypes (atmosphere LUTs, sky, tonemap, auto exposure, GTAO,
  CSM with PCF/PCSS, height fog, sky ambient SH, stylised lighting). All pass naga 30.0.1 (section 9).
- `docs/research/lighting-shaders/reference/*.py`: a numpy reference of the same maths (sky LUTs, tonemappers,
  12 presets, a small box-scene renderer).
- `docs/agents/37-lighting/*.png` and two tables: the reference renders I looked at and tuned against.

## 1. Summary

1. **Physical sky from Hillaire 2020.** Four small LUTs (transmittance, multi-scattering, sky-view, aerial
   perspective) cost 0.31 ms at 720p on a GTX 1080 and about 1 ms on an iPhone 6s (Sourced, paper). They give
   correct sunsets, twilight and sun colour from two creator inputs: `ClockTime` and `GeographicLatitude`.
   The numpy port in this PR reproduces the expected curves (section 3.1, `sky_elevations.png`).
2. **Exposure in real units, metered by a histogram.** Sun 100-120k lux, moon about 0.25 lux. A 256-bin luminance
   histogram (0.17 ms at 1080p on an RTX 2080, Sourced) meters the scene; creators get `ExposureCompensation`
   and a night bias so night still reads as night.
3. **Tonemapper "Default" = AgX with the Punchy look** (assistant's call). ACES skews reds to orange and blues to
   magenta (Sourced, Bevy docs; visible in `tonemap_hues.png`). PBR Neutral stays the curve for Soft, Toon and
   Pixel looks, as render-styles planned.
4. **GI without a bake.** Parts move, so baked lightmaps do not fit. Ladder: sky SH + voxel sky visibility (Low),
   DDGI-style probes traced against a voxelised Parts grid (Medium, High), hardware ray queries (Ultra).
5. **Shadows: keep CSM, add a blend band, ShadowSoftness, PCSS (High) and contact shadows.** VSM rejected.
6. **Creator API keeps Roblox names** (`Lighting`, `Atmosphere`, `Sky`, `Clouds`, `BloomEffect`,
   `ColorCorrectionEffect`, `ColorGradingEffect`) with defaults that look good untouched, plus a few Metallic
   additions (`HeightFalloff`, `AirColor`, `NightAdaptation`). 12 presets with exact values in section 6.
7. **Eight cheap hooks for Task #3 now** (section 8.1). Two matter most: an HDR target with a finish pass
   (also render-styles hook 1) and a CPU sky evaluation that feeds today's gradient uniforms from `ClockTime`.

## 2. What Task 3 has today (Sourced: `crates/metallic_render` on `origin/main`, read only)

- Forward wgpu renderer. One sun, 3 cascades at 2048 (High) or 1 at 1024 (Low), Depth32Float array,
  `SHADOW_DISTANCE` 400 studs, split lambda 0.7, 3x3 PCF, normal offset 2.5 texels, fade from 85% to 100%.
- Sky is a three-colour gradient (`Sky { zenith, horizon, ground, to_sun, sun_color, fog_density, exposure }`)
  that also serves as ambient light. Fog is exp² with density 1/900.
- `finish()` in `scene.wgsl` applies exposure, the Narkowicz ACES fit and sRGB with dither, writing straight to the
  swapchain. No HDR target, no post passes.
- 4x MSAA on High. `Quality` is Low or High. The `Lighting` class exists in `metallic_core` with no properties.

The renderer is clean and small, which makes the M1 hooks cheap. The gap to "better than Roblox Future" is in
inputs (no time of day, no exposure, gradient ambient) more than in shading.

## 3. Research findings

### 3.1 Sky and atmosphere

- Hillaire 2020 splits the sky into a 256x64 transmittance LUT (40 steps, 0.01 ms), a 32x32 multi-scattering LUT
  (20 steps, 64 directions, 0.07 ms), a 200x100 sky-view LUT (30 steps, 0.05 ms) and a 32³ aerial-perspective
  volume (30 steps, 0.04 ms). Total 0.31 ms at 1280x720 on a GTX 1080, plus 0.14 ms to draw. On an iPhone 6s the
  sky-view LUT runs at 96x50 with 8 steps in 0.27 ms. (Sourced, paper table 2.)
- Multiple scattering uses an infinite geometric series, Psi = L2 / (1 - f_ms), so one LUT covers all orders.
  Mie phase is Cornette-Shanks with g = 0.8; scale heights 8 km (Rayleigh) and 1.2 km (Mie); ozone a 30 km tent at
  25 km. (Sourced, paper and MIT reference code `RenderSkyCommon.hlsl`.)
- Earth constants used here come from the reference code: Rayleigh (0.005802, 0.013558, 0.033100)/km, Mie
  scattering 0.003996, extinction 0.004440, ozone (0.000650, 0.001881, 0.000085), radii 6360/6460 km.
  (Sourced, `GameRender.cpp`.)
- Bevy 0.16 ships a Hillaire atmosphere but it does not yet light the scene: the environment map and direct light
  ignore it (Sourced, Bevy 0.16 notes). Metallic should close that loop from day one: the same LUTs set sun colour,
  sky ambient and fog colour (Judgment). That is the main visual win over a gradient sky.
- The numpy port (`reference/atmosphere.py`) gives these values (`sky_table.md`, generated): sun at the ground at
  45° elevation is (0.913, 0.815, 0.68) of the top-of-atmosphere value; at 5° it is (0.519, 0.236, 0.06).
  Sky share of ground illuminance is 0.11 at 45° and 0.52 at 5°. Filament measures about 0.17-0.2 for a real
  clear day (Sourced, Filament: sun 100-105k lux, sky 20-25k lux), so real air holds more aerosol than the default
  model. `Haze` default 1 adds 80% Mie on top to close part of that gap (Judgment).

### 3.2 Sun, moon, stars and time of day

- Solar declination is −23.44°·cos(360/365·(N + 10)) (Sourced, Wikipedia). Metallic fixes the date at an equinox
  (declination 0) so sunrise is 06:00 and sunset 18:00 at any latitude (assistant's call: creators expect
  `ClockTime 18` to be sunset, and Roblox keeps no date either).
- World frame: x east, y up, z north. Sun direction from hour angle h and latitude φ:
  with h = 15°·(ClockTime − 12) and declination 0, `dir = (−sin h, cos φ·cos h, −sin φ·cos h)` (east, up, north),
  so the noon sun stands south of the zenith in the northern hemisphere. Moon opposite the sun (full moon)
  (Judgment, `reference/render.py` `celestial_dirs`).
- Illuminance: sun 120,000 lux above the atmosphere = renderer unit 1 when `Brightness` = 1. Full moon about 1 lux
  in Filament (Sourced); I use 0.25 lux × `MoonBrightness`, which reads better after the night bias (Judgment).
- Sun disk: real-sun luminance with limb darkening 0.6, cut at the horizon, drawn at `SunAngularSize` (default
  1.5°, visual only; the real sun is 0.53°). Shadows use `ShadowSoftness` instead (Judgment).
- Stars: hashed per direction on a sphere, 3000 by default, twinkle from time, faded by sky luminance so they show
  only at night (Judgment, `sky.wgsl`).

### 3.3 Shadows

- Cascaded shadow maps stay. VSM and EVSM need wide formats and leak light behind thin Parts; CSM with PCF and a
  depth bias already works in Task 3 (Judgment).
- PCSS: blocker search, then penumbra width w = (d_receiver − d_blocker)·w_light/d_blocker, then PCF of that width
  (Sourced, NVIDIA PCSS paper). For a sun the light is at infinity, so w = gap·2·tan(angular radius) (Judgment).
  The 2005 demo ran 36 blocker + 64 PCF taps; the prototype uses 16 gathers + 16 taps with a rotated Vogel disk
  (Judgment, `shadows_csm.wgsl`).
- Contact shadows: Bend Studio's screen-space shadows (Days Gone) trace a short ray in the depth buffer per pixel
  and complement the shadow map for small details (Sourced). On Parts they fix the "floating" look of thin
  objects where the cascade texel is too coarse (Judgment).
- Cascade transitions: a dithered blend band (10% of each cascade) removes the visible seam; MSAA or TAA hides the
  dither (Judgment).
- `ShadowSoftness` maps to a light radius: max(0.27°, 3°·ShadowSoftness). Default 0.2 gives 0.6°. Roblox's
  `ShadowSoftness` defaults to 0.2 and only works with Realistic lighting (Sourced, Roblox Lighting docs).

### 3.4 Global illumination without a bake

- Roblox `GlobalShadows` computes sky visibility on 4x4x4-stud voxels (Sourced, Roblox Lighting docs). That is the
  Low-tier baseline to beat.
- Filament stores irradiance in 9 SH coefficients (or 4 on low-end) and adds specular and horizon occlusion
  (Sourced). `sky_ambient.wgsl` projects the sky-view LUT plus a ground bounce onto SH9 each frame in about 0.01 ms
  (Judgment, 1024 samples in one workgroup).
- DDGI (Majercik et al. 2019) stores irradiance and depth moments in octahedral probes on a grid and updates them
  with rays each frame (Judgment from memory; the JCGT page returned no content). Without RT hardware, rays can
  march a voxelised copy of the Parts grid. Parts are boxes, wedges and cylinders, so a 1-stud or 2-stud occupancy
  grid captures them well (Judgment).
- Radiance cascades (Sannikov) trade angular for spatial resolution by distance and need no denoiser; Path of Exile 2
  uses a screen-space 2D version, and the paper discusses light leaks (Sourced, paper source). A 3D world-space
  version is still research; keep it as an Ultra experiment (Judgment).
- SSGI is cheap but loses everything off screen and flickers when a Part leaves the view (Judgment). Use it only as
  a short-range detail on top of probes, if at all.
- SDF GI (signed distance fields of Parts) is a good fit for primitives but costs a field rebuild on edits; DDGI on
  voxels gives most of the benefit with simpler updates (Judgment).

### 3.5 Reflections

- Thief (Advances 2014) layered reflections: SSR, then local cubemaps, then a global fallback (Sourced, talk list).
  Metallic order: SSR (High) → box-projected reflection probes (Medium+) → sky-view LUT as the global fallback
  (all tiers) (Judgment).
- Specular occlusion from AO (Lagarde) and horizon occlusion keep reflections from glowing inside crevices
  (Sourced, Filament). `gtao.wgsl` includes the specular occlusion term.

### 3.6 Ambient occlusion

- XeGTAO High is 3 slices × 6 samples: 0.56 ms at 1080p on an RTX 2060, 2.39 ms on an i7-1195G7 (Iris Xe), 1.4 ms
  at 4K on an RTX 3070. Medium costs about 2/3 of High; bent normals add 25%. MIT licence; the repo is archived.
  (Sourced, XeGTAO README.)
- On Iris Xe that means half resolution with 2 slices at most, or off (Judgment). The multi-bounce fit keeps bright
  albedos from going grey (`gtao.wgsl`, Judgment).

### 3.7 Fog and volumetrics

- Analytic exponential height fog: density σ(y) = σ0·exp(−k·(y − h)) integrates in closed form along a ray, so it
  costs a few ALU ops per pixel (Judgment). Unreal's Exponential Height Fog exposes the same knobs: density,
  height falloff and a second layer with its own height offset (Sourced, Unreal docs). `height_fog.wgsl` colours the fog from the sky-view LUT at the horizon and adds Henyey-Greenstein sun
  in-scatter, so fog matches the sky at every time of day.
- Wronski's volumetric fog uses 160x90x64 (or 128) froxels with exponential depth slices, about 1.1 ms total in
  2014 (Sourced, SIGGRAPH 2014 slides). It gives shadowed light shafts and lights inside fog. High tier (Judgment).
- Roblox `SunRays` is a screen-space post effect (Sourced, post-processing docs). Volumetric fog replaces it on High;
  the name maps to the sun in-scatter strength (Judgment).

### 3.8 Local lights and emissives

- Clustered shading: a 16x9x24 froxel grid with DOOM-style log depth slices, 100 lights per cluster in the sample
  (Sourced, Ortiz). Fits forward rendering and MSAA (Judgment), which matches render-styles' "clustered forward" call.
- Roblox clamps PointLight, SpotLight and SurfaceLight range at 120 studs (Sourced). Metallic keeps that cap for
  compatibility and windows the falloff to Range: E = I/max(d², 0.01²)·(1 − d⁴/r⁴)² (Sourced form, Filament).
- Units: `PointLight.Brightness` 1 = 1000 candela, distance in metres at 0.28 m/stud (assistant's call; puts a
  Brightness 1 lamp at the right strength next to a 120k lux sun once exposure adapts).
- Neon material: exposure-relative emissive at 2.8 × albedo, 1.5 EV above display white, so it blooms the same at
  noon and at night (assistant's call). Emissives do not light the scene until GI probes pick them up (Medium+).

### 3.9 Exposure

- Physical camera: EV100 = log2(N²/t) − log2(S/100), exposure = 1/(1.2·2^EV100) (Sourced, Filament). Pre-exposing
  lights before shading keeps fp16 targets in range (Sourced, Filament).
- Tardif's histogram: 256 bins over log2 luminance −10..2, 16x16 thread groups, adaptation with time constant 1.1,
  0.17 ms at 1080p on an RTX 2080 (Sourced).
- Metallic metering (assistant's call, `exposure_histogram.wgsl`): centre-weighted (weight 1 at edges to 4 at
  centre), average over the 30%-95% percentile band, EV100 clamped to [`ExposureMin`, `ExposureMax`] = [−4, 16].
  Night bias −2 EV × (1 − smoothstep(0, 10, EV)) × (0.5 + 0.5·`NightAdaptation`). Faster adaptation to light than
  to dark.
- Night tint (Purkinje-like): mix toward luma·(0.72, 0.82, 1.0) with strength
  `NightAdaptation`·0.7·(1 − smoothstep(−3, 5, EV)) (Judgment). Moonlit renders blue-grey, as people remember it.
- Godot multiplies exposure by 2.0 for Filmic and 1.8 for ACES so mid-grey matches across curves (Sourced, Godot
  docs). Metallic does the same with measured constants so scene 0.18 lands on one display value for every curve:
  AgX 1.0, AgX Punchy 1.7316, PBR Neutral 1.4137, ACES (Hill) 1.6752, Narkowicz 0.8306, Clamp 1.0 (Judgment, computed
  in `reference/tonemap.py`). Switching curves then changes contrast and hue, not brightness.

### 3.10 Tonemapping and grading

- AgX (minimal polynomial fit; iolite engine) maps log2 exposure −12.47..4.03 through a 6th-order polynomial with
  inset/outset matrices and supports looks; Punchy = power 1.35, saturation 1.4 (Sourced, iolite repository).
- Khronos PBR Neutral keeps base colours exact up to 0.76, then compresses highlights, desaturating them by 0.15
  (Sourced, Khronos README, Apache-2.0). Bevy calls it "highly saturated" and notes it "crushes grays" (Sourced).
- ACES (Hill fit, MIT, BakingLab) and the Narkowicz fit shift reds toward orange and blues toward magenta at high
  exposure (Sourced, Bevy docs). `tonemap_hues.png` shows it: a pure blue ramp turns purple under both ACES fits.
- Tony McMapface is a neutral LUT-based curve, MIT/Apache, Bevy's default (Sourced). Good, but needs a 3D LUT
  texture; AgX gives a similar result from ALU only (Judgment).
- **Decision (assistant's call): `TonemapperPreset.Default` = AgX Punchy for the Realistic look.** This changes
  render-styles' "ACES fit for Realistic". Reasons: no hue skew, smooth highlight roll-off to white for the sun disk
  and Neon, and Punchy restores the saturation plain AgX loses. `tonemap_scenes.png` compares the four curves on
  three presets. `Retro` (Roblox's other preset name) = clamp, for the PS1/flat looks.
- Grading runs in scene-linear space before the curve: white balance tint, saturation, contrast around mid-grey,
  brightness, vignette (Judgment). A 32³ LUT slot after the curve takes creator LUTs later (render-styles 5.4).

### 3.11 Bloom

- Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare bloom uses a pyramid of downsamples and upsamples for temporal stability; the
  first downsample weights each sample by 1/(1 + luma) to stop fireflies (Sourced, Jimenez article and its
  replies). The no-threshold blend with a lerp of about 0.04 and the 3x3 tent upsample come from the talk slides,
  which I could not open (Judgment from memory). The result is energy-conserving and stable (Judgment). `BloomEffect.Intensity` maps to the lerp; Metallic default 0.04.
  `Threshold` stays for Roblox compatibility but defaults to 0 (Judgment).

### 3.12 Anti-aliasing for hard-edged Parts

- Parts have long straight silhouettes that crawl under motion. MSAA 4x solves geometry edges at the source and is
  in Task 3 already (Sourced, Task 3 code).
- TAA: 4-8 jitter samples, 10% history blend, neighbourhood clamping, Catmull-Rom history fetch (Sourced, López).
  Needed for Ultra effects that accumulate noise (PCSS, GTAO, SSR); it blurs Part edges unless sharpened (Judgment).
- SMAA 1x/T2x/S2x/4x modes (Sourced, iryoku). Low tier: SMAA 1x if its lookup textures fit the budget, FXAA as the
  fallback (assistant's call; render-styles listed FXAA/CMAA2, either is fine).
- Medium/High: MSAA 4x plus per-effect temporal accumulation (GTAO, PCSS noise) without full-screen TAA (Judgment).
  Ultra: TAA + FSR 2 (render-styles 9).

### 3.13 Roblox API surface (Sourced: Roblox engine docs for Lighting, Atmosphere, Sky, post-processing)

- `Lighting`: `Ambient` default black, `OutdoorAmbient` (127,127,127), `EnvironmentDiffuseScale` and
  `EnvironmentSpecularScale` 0..1 default 0, `ExposureCompensation` −5..5 default 0, `ShadowSoftness` 0.2,
  `GlobalShadows`, `LightingStyle` Realistic/Soft, `PrioritizeLightingQuality`; `Technology` is deprecated.
- `Sky`: `SunAngularSize` default 21 (0..60), `CelestialBodiesShown` true, skybox faces, star count.
- `Atmosphere`: `Density`, `Offset`, `Color`, `Decay`, `Glare`, `Haze`. The page gives descriptions, not defaults.
  The defaults I use for the Roblox mapping (Density 0.395, Offset 0.25, Color (199,199,199), Decay
  (106,112,125)) come from memory: unverified.
- Post effects: `BloomEffect`, `BlurEffect`, `ColorCorrectionEffect` (Brightness, Contrast, Saturation,
  TintColor), `DepthOfFieldEffect`, `SunRaysEffect`, `ColorGradingEffect` with `TonemapperPreset` Default/Retro.

### 3.14 Per-style lighting

- Render-styles owns materials and outlines; lighting has to feed every Look from one `Lighting` setup (Judgment).
  `stylized_lighting.wgsl` holds the functions:
  - **Soft** (`LightingStyle.Soft`): wrap diffuse (w 0.3), light radius floor 1.5°, PBR Neutral.
  - **Toon**: combine shadow map and N·L first, then band them, so cast shadows and the terminator share one edge;
    flatten ambient toward the hemisphere average; tint the shade side.
  - **Pixel 3D**: toon bands, then quantise the light (in 1 EV steps), not the albedo, so the palette survives.
  - **Flat / low-poly**: faceted normal from screen derivatives, computed before any branch.
- Stylised looks skip GTAO's lit-side darkening and use hard or tent shadows, never PCSS (Judgment).

## 5. Creator Lighting API

Rules (assistant's call):

- Roblox names and ranges stay, so scripts and imported places keep working. Metallic additions are marked +.
- Defaults are physical and look right untouched: a new place shows a clear 14:30 afternoon (`presets.png`, Clear
  Day), not the grey-violet void Roblox starts with.
- If a place has no `Atmosphere`, the sky stays physical (Earth air) with `Density` 0 (no ground fog). The new-place
  template inserts `Atmosphere`, `Sky`, `Clouds` and a `ColorGradingEffect` at defaults so creators can see the
  knobs (assistant's call).
- Ambient terms are exposure-relative: colour 255 adds pre-exposed radiance 0.5 on a white surface, whatever the
  time of day. A creator who sets `Ambient` to (60,45,80) gets the same visible lift at noon and at midnight.

### 5.1 Lighting

| Property | Default | Range | Meaning in Metallic |
|---|---|---|---|
| ClockTime / TimeOfDay | 14.5 | 0..24 | Sun and moon position (equinox, 06:00 sunrise) |
| GeographicLatitude | 41.73 | −90..90 | Sun path tilt |
| Brightness | 1 | 0..10 | Direct sun scale; 1 = 120,000 lux above the air. Sky stays physical |
| ExposureCompensation | 0 | −5..5 | EV added after metering |
| + AutoExposure | true | bool | Off: fixed EV100 from the sun elevation (5.5) |
| + ExposureMin / ExposureMax | −4 / 16 | EV100 | Metering clamp |
| + NightAdaptation | 1 | 0..1 | Night bias strength and blue-grey night tint |
| + MoonBrightness | 1 | 0..10 | Moon = 0.25 lux × value |
| Ambient | (0,0,0) | Color3 | Exposure-relative flat light everywhere, also lights fog (city glow) |
| OutdoorAmbient | (0,0,0) | Color3 | Exposure-relative, multiplied by sky visibility |
| ColorShift_Top | (0,0,0) | Color3 | Sun tint: sun × (1 + colour) |
| ColorShift_Bottom | (0,0,0) | Color3 | Ground bounce tint |
| EnvironmentDiffuseScale | 1 | 0..1 | Sky and probe diffuse |
| EnvironmentSpecularScale | 1 | 0..1 | Sky, probe and SSR reflections |
| GlobalShadows | true | bool | Sun shadows and sky visibility |
| ShadowSoftness | 0.2 | 0..1 | Light radius max(0.27°, 3°·value) |
| LightingStyle | Realistic | Realistic/Soft | Soft: wrap diffuse, 1.5° floor, PBR Neutral |
| PrioritizeLightingQuality | false | bool | Lets the tier system keep shadows and GI before resolution |

Differences from Roblox: `OutdoorAmbient` 0 instead of 127 and both `Environment*Scale` at 1 instead of 0, because
the physical sky already supplies ambient and reflections. The Roblox importer maps `OutdoorAmbient` 127 to 0 and
keeps other values; it maps `Environment*Scale` 0 to 1 only for places that used the default (assistant's call).

### 5.5 Exposure modes

- `AutoExposure` true: histogram metering (section 3.9).

  creators a stable image for cutscenes; `ExposureCompensation` still applies.

## 7. Shaders: what each prototype does

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `atmosphere_common.wgsl` | Parameters, medium sampling, phase functions, LUT mappings, the shared ray integrator |
| `atmosphere_transmittance.wgsl` | 256x64 transmittance LUT, 40 steps, 8x8 groups |
| `atmosphere_multiscatter.wgsl` | 32x32 multi-scattering LUT, 64 directions per texel in one workgroup, tree reduction |
| `atmosphere_skyview.wgsl` | 192x108 sky-view LUT with sun and moon |
| `sky.wgsl` | Sky draw at the reverse-Z far plane: LUT, overcast blend, sun disk, moon, stars |
| `sky_ambient.wgsl` | Sky + ground bounce to SH9 (cosine-convolved) and the lookup function |
| `tonemap.wgsl` | Finish pass: exposure, grade, night tint, AgX/Punchy, PBR Neutral, ACES Hill, Narkowicz, clamp, vignette, sRGB, dither |
| `exposure_histogram.wgsl` | 256-bin centre-weighted histogram, percentile metering, adaptation, night bias |
| `gtao.wgsl` | Horizon-based GTAO, depth-aware 3x3 denoise, multi-bounce, specular occlusion |
| `shadows_csm.wgsl` | Cascade pick with dithered blend, normal-offset bias, tent PCF, PCSS |
| `height_fog.wgsl` | Analytic height fog with sky-coloured ambient and HG sun/moon in-scatter; forward function and full-screen pass |
| `stylized_lighting.wgsl` | Soft wrap, toon bands, toon ambient, light quantisation, faceted normals |

The atmosphere shaders follow the paper's maths and my numpy port; I wrote them fresh (no code copied from the
MIT reference; its constants are data). The tonemap curves use the published constants of AgX (iolite), Khronos
PBR Neutral (Apache-2.0) and the Hill ACES fit (MIT).

## 8. Phased plan

### 8.1 M1, urgent cheap hooks for Task #3

Ordered by value. Items 1, 4 and 7 overlap render-styles section 13; the rest are lighting-specific.

1. **HDR target and finish pass** (render-styles hook 1). Without it, bloom, exposure metering and curve choice
   cannot exist.
2. **CPU physical sky feeding today's uniforms.** Add `ClockTime` and `GeographicLatitude` and compute `to_sun`,
   `sun_color`, `zenith`, `horizon` and `ground` from them each frame. `docs/agents/37-lighting/m1_sky_table.md`
   lists the values per sun elevation in today's renderer units (45° sun = today's `sun_color` luminance); linear
   interpolation in elevation is enough for M1. About 40 lines of Rust and a 17-row table. No shader change.
3. **Exposure separated from the curve, with pre-exposure.** Keep `exposure` as a uniform, set it from the table's
   exposure column (1.0 at 45°, 12 at sunset, thousands at night) times 2^`ExposureCompensation`. The GPU histogram
   replaces the table in M2.
4. **Tonemap curve index in the finish uniform** (render-styles hook 2). Ship AgX Punchy as Default with the
   normalisation constant 1.7316; keep Narkowicz as `Filmic`.
5. **Height fog replacing exp² fog.** Same cost; `fog_density` becomes σ0 and falloff, and fog colour comes from
   the horizon colour (today's uniform) plus sun in-scatter. `height_fog.wgsl` has the function.
6. **ShadowSoftness and a cascade blend band.** `ShadowSoftness` scales the PCF radius; a 10% dithered blend band
   hides the cascade seams. Both are a few lines in `scene.wgsl`.
7. **Sampleable depth** (render-styles hook 4). GTAO, contact shadows, fog pass and SSR need it later.
8. **A `Lighting` struct with Roblox names** replacing `Sky` in the renderer API (`clock_time`, `brightness`,
   `exposure_compensation`, `ambient`, `outdoor_ambient`, `shadow_softness`, `fog: Atmosphere`). Studio and Luau
   then bind to stable names.

None changes M1's tests in a way that needs new hardware; hooks 2, 3 and 5 change the look at times other than
mid-afternoon, which is the point.

### 8.2 M2 (render-styles puts "Hillaire sky + time of day (with #37)" here)

- `Lighting`, `Atmosphere`, `Sky`, `Clouds` and the post effect classes with properties in `metallic_core`.
- GPU sky LUTs, sky draw, sun disk, moon, stars; sky ambient SH9.
- Histogram auto exposure, AgX/Neutral/ACES/Retro curves, grade, bloom.
- Analytic height fog with sky colour.
- Voxel sky visibility (4-stud grid, CPU-built from Parts, uploaded as a 3D texture) times sky SH: the Low-tier GI
  that matches Roblox `GlobalShadows` and beats it with a coloured physical sky.
- The 12 presets as a Studio picker; a headless test that renders each preset and checks mean luminance and hue
  stay in a band.

### 8.3 M5 renderer track (#18)

- Shadows v2: 4 cascades, PCSS on High, contact shadows, cached static far cascade.
- GTAO with temporal accumulation.
- DDGI-style probes on a voxelised Parts grid, with probe visibility on High.
- Clustered forward lights (render-styles issue 7) with shadowed spot/point lights in an atlas.
- Reflection probes, SSR with probe fallback.
- Volumetric froxel fog with shadowed sun shafts; `SunRaysEffect` maps onto it.
- Aerial perspective LUT applied to distant geometry.

### 8.4 Ultra (later)

Ray-query probes and reflections, world-space radiance cascades experiment, TAA + FSR 2 as the default AA.

## 10. Proposed issues (not created)

These avoid repeating render-styles section 14.

1. **Lighting service with Roblox-named properties.** Add `Lighting`, `Atmosphere`, `Sky`, `Clouds` and post
   effect properties to `metallic_core` with the defaults in section 5; Luau and Studio bind to them.
2. **Physical sky: Hillaire LUTs, sun, moon, stars, time of day.** Port the four prototypes; drive sun colour, sky
   ambient and fog colour from the LUTs so the whole frame agrees.
3. **Auto exposure and pre-exposed lighting.** Histogram metering, EV100 units, night bias and tint; light units
   (lux, candela) documented for creators.
4. **Tonemap curves and grading in the finish pass.** AgX Punchy default, Neutral, ACES, Filmic, Retro with mid-grey
   normalisation; scene-linear grade; LUT slot. Merges with render-styles issue 4 if that lands first.
5. **Height fog with sky colour.** Replace exp² fog; expose `HeightFalloff`, `FogHeight`, `Absorption`; per-pixel in
   the forward shader so transparents fog correctly.
6. **Low-tier GI: sky SH9 times voxel sky visibility.** 4-stud occupancy grid from Parts, updated on edits; sky
   visibility cone-traced per probe; beats Roblox `GlobalShadows` at the same grid size.
7. **Shadows v2: blend band, ShadowSoftness, PCSS, contact shadows, cached far cascade.** Tier-specific modes from
   `shadows_csm.wgsl`; screen-space contact shadows on High.
8. **Runtime DDGI on a voxelised Parts grid (no bake).** Revises render-styles issue 9, which bakes probes in
   Studio; moving Parts need runtime updates. Octahedral irradiance + depth moments, amortised ray budget.
9. **Reflections: reflection probes and SSR with fallback.** Box-projected probes refreshed on change, half-res SSR
   on High, sky-view LUT as the global fallback.
10. **Volumetric fog and light shafts.** Froxel grid with shadowed sun and local lights; maps `SunRaysEffect`.
11. **Lighting presets in Studio with a regression test.** Picker for the 12 presets; headless renders compared
    against stored luminance and hue bands per preset.
12. **GPU-vs-numpy check for the sky LUTs.** Run the WGSL LUT shaders headless and compare them to
    `reference/atmosphere.py` within a tolerance; catches mapping bugs naga cannot.
